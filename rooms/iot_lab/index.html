DIFF

What differs from the photos (ranked)

  1. Round 1 fixed: brightness now matches the photos (window not blown out), clerestory glass reads as daylight, darker floor with visible studs, clear partition grid, louvre band and whiter blinds, posters raised to just under the ceiling with flat colours, open chairs, pale-green lockers below the ceiling, cameras moved back to the doorway and the south-east corner.
  2. 1. Chairs still read too light. The open mesh is half transparent, so against the bright room the backs look grey and the silver frames dominate; in the photos they are clearly black mesh shells with curved backs on silver legs. The frame is also rectilinear where the real one is curved.
  3. 2. Foreground still crowded in two frames. 02: the nearest bench desk with its monitors fills about a third of the lower left. 04: the round table takes about a quarter of the lower left and two chairs fill the right edge. This is the room size: the model room is about 0.7x the real one, and the cameras already sit in the doorway and the corner.
  4. 3. The room looks shallower than in the photos. Photo 42 looks down about 6 m to the window; the model gives 4.3 m, so the TV and the window wall look close and large.
  5. 4. Poster headlines are readable but small and light; the photographic artwork (eye, gears, robot arm, faces) is reduced to one flat disc per poster on purpose.
  6. 5. Lockers read nearly white under the brighter light; the pale green is subtle compared with photo 48.
  7. 6. Boards and screens are plain: no handwriting, no A4 poster, no image on the TV (clutter left out on purpose).
  8. 7. PC towers on the bench are plain black boxes; in the photos they are smaller towers with visible front panels, and laptops sit on the desks (left out with the clutter).
