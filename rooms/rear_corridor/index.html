Zone: corridor X -1.77..+0.61, Y -3.16..+1.28 between the ground-floor washrooms (Reception -> rear door), soffit 2.14 m; Auditorium north face Y -5.03 opposite the rear door. DIFF

What differs from the photos (ranked)

  1. Corridor light: the existing Reception scene has no ceiling lights south of the Reception, so the corridor and its murals read darker than the phone photos (no light was added: walls only).
  2. The Auditorium wall is rendered from the corridor scene, through the rear door: the existing outside scene renders the covered passage almost black (the model's passage is 1.78 m wide under a canopy; the real one ~4 m).
  3. The dark grey pilasters in the Auditorium photo are part of the picture (flat), not raised from the wall.
  4. Each photo fills its wall's full height: the model's corridor soffit is 2.14 m (real ~3 m). Typewriter photo extended 0.41 m at the north end with its own grey wall; cyborg photo trimmed 0.47 m of plain wall at the south end; the black floor strip cut off the typewriter photo.
  5. The photos' own lighting is part of the texture (the bright patch on the plain wall right of the cyborg).
